 The study area is Hawaii from November to December 31st of the year 2025. I chose Hawaii because that is my home state and was interested how ships move around. I concluded that one month is enough to show the pattern in ship navigation. This way the data is more manageable as well.
With very few exceptions most tanker and cargo stops at honolulu harbor. Not even the big island has any ports with a reliable traffic.This is because the Pearl harbor area is the only bay that is deep enough for a big cargo ship. Most coast in hawaii ends shallow. Info on harbor: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honolulu_Harbor
This visualization is useful for the people in Hawaii. Analyst can use this information to figure out trade inputs. Additionally environmentallist can use this data as well. Unfortunately oil spill is very common around there, and this data can be used to implement preventative measure.
